ABOUT THE INSTITUTION

Alamuri Ratnamala Institute of Engineering & Technology

ARMIET, established under the Koti Vidya Charitable Trust, is a premier engineering institution affiliated with the University of Mumbai and approved by AICTE, New Delhi & DTE Maharashtra (DTE Code: 3219).

The CampusConnect wayfinding project serves our student community, visiting recruiters, examiners, and guests with precision navigation across academic departments, research hubs, and administrative wings.
